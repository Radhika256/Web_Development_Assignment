Rules

  • College ID card is mandatory.
  • Participants must report 30 minutes before the event.
  • Participants must follow coordinator instructions.
  • Unfair practices may lead to disqualification.
  • Maintain discipline and cleanliness at the venue.

Important Terms

Open Event
An event open to eligible participants from different colleges.
Team Event
An event in which participants compete as a group.
Individual Event
An event in which one participant competes alone.
Registration
The process of enrolling for a fest event.
Coordinator
The person responsible for managing an event.
Final Round
The last round used to decide the winners.
