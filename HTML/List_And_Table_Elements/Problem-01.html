University Admission Handbook

Welcome to the GLA University Admission Handbook. This handbook provides important information about admission, eligibility, application steps, required documents, admission terms, fee structure and contact details.

(The Admission Office of GLA University welcomes aspiring students and encourages them to carefully read all admission requirements before beginning the application process.)

Admission Overview

GLA University provides undergraduate, postgraduate and other academic programmes for students seeking quality education and professional development. Candidates should check the eligibility requirements and complete the admission process within the prescribed deadline.

Mandatory requirement: Applicants must provide correct academic and personal information and submit all required documents during the admission process.

Application Deadline: 30 June 2027

Eligibility

Students must satisfy the eligibility conditions specified for their selected programme. The required qualification may differ according to the course.

  • Applicants must have completed the required qualifying examination.
  • Applicants must meet the minimum academic requirements of the selected programme.
  • Applicants must provide valid academic records.
  • Applicants must satisfy programme-specific admission requirements.
  • Applicants must complete the application before the prescribed deadline.

Application Process

The admission steps must be completed in the following sequence:

  1. Visit the official GLA University admission website.
  2. Select the desired programme or course.
  3. Register by providing the required personal information.
  4. Complete the online application form.
  5. Upload the required documents.
  6. Pay the applicable application or admission fee.
  7. Submit the completed application form.
  8. Wait for the admission decision, merit list or counselling information.
  9. Complete the admission formalities after selection.

Required Documents

The following documents may be required during the admission process. Their order does not matter.

  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • Class 10 mark sheet and certificate
  • Class 12 mark sheet and certificate
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Migration Certificate, where applicable
  • Government-issued identity proof
  • Category certificate, where applicable
  • Entrance examination scorecard, where applicable

Important Terms

Eligibility
The academic and other requirements that a student must satisfy to apply for a particular programme.
Merit List
A list of candidates prepared according to their academic performance or admission criteria.
Counselling
The process through which eligible or selected students receive guidance regarding programmes, seats and admission.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students according to the applicable scholarship rules.
Registration
The process of officially entering a student's details into the university admission system.
