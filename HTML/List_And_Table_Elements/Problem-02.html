Ember & Spice

A warm and welcoming restaurant serving delicious Indian and international flavours made with fresh ingredients and traditional cooking techniques.

Starters

  • Smoky Paneer Tikka :Char-grilled cottage cheese with peppers and aromatic spices.
  • Golden Vegetable Samosa : Crispy pastry filled with seasoned vegetables.
  • Classic Chicken Seekh Kebab: Spiced minced chicken grilled over an open flame.

Main Course

  • Ember Butter Chicken : Tender chicken cooked in a creamy tomato and butter gravy.
  • Royal Paneer Lababdar : Cottage cheese cooked with tomato, onion and Indian spices.
  • Herb Grilled Chicken : Grilled chicken served with herbs and seasonal vegetables.

Desserts

  • Chocolate Lava Cake : Warm chocolate cake with a rich molten centre.
  • Gulab Jamun : Soft milk-solid dumplings served in sweet syrup.

Beverages

  • Masala Chai : Traditional Indian tea prepared with aromatic spices.
  • Fresh Mango Cooler : Chilled mango drink with a refreshing citrus touch.
  • Classic Cold Coffee : Creamy chilled coffee topped with foam.

Chef's Recommended Dining Experience

  1. Begin with our Smoky Paneer Tikka starter.
  2. Enjoy our Ember Butter Chicken with freshly prepared bread.
  3. Refresh yourself with a Fresh Mango Cooler.
  4. Finish your meal with our Chocolate Lava Cake.
