Preparation Guide

Notice: Keep digital badge handy for entry.

Tips

  • Bring 10+ resume copies.
  • Prepare a quick elevator pitch.
  • Dress professionally.

Attendee Journey

  1. Register online to get Reference ID.
  2. Print entry pass.
  3. Visit recruiter booths and submit resumes.
