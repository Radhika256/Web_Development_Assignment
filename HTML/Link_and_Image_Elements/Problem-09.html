About Us

Welcome to Mocha Haven Café, a friendly and comfortable café where coffee lovers and food enthusiasts can enjoy fresh food and refreshing drinks. Our café is designed to provide a relaxing environment for students, families, friends and professionals.

We believe that a good café is more than just a place to eat and drink. It is a place where people can relax, have conversations, work, study and create memorable moments. We prepare our food with fresh ingredients and serve our beverages with care.

From a freshly brewed cup of coffee in the morning to a delicious dessert in the evening, Mocha Haven Café offers something for everyone. Our friendly service and comfortable atmosphere make every visit enjoyable.

Signature Items

Our signature menu includes some of our most popular food and drinks. These items are prepared fresh and are perfect for breakfast, snacks or casual meetings with friends.

1. Classic Cappuccino

Our Classic Cappuccino is made with rich espresso, steamed milk and smooth creamy foam. It has a balanced coffee flavor and is one of our most popular beverages.

2. Chocolate Brownie

Our Chocolate Brownie is a soft and delicious dessert with a rich chocolate flavor. It is an excellent choice for anyone looking for a sweet treat with their coffee.

3. Grilled Vegetable Sandwich

The Grilled Vegetable Sandwich is prepared with fresh vegetables, melted cheese and crispy toasted bread. It is a tasty and filling choice for breakfast, lunch or an evening snack.
