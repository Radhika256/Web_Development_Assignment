GLA University

About

GLA University is located in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, India. The university provides multidisciplinary education and offers programmes in areas such as Engineering, Management, Computer Applications, Pharmacy, Law, Education, Science and other professional fields.

The university focuses on academic learning, practical knowledge, research, innovation and the overall development of students. GLA University has a large campus and provides laboratories, library facilities, hostels and opportunities for extracurricular activities.

Programs

GLA University offers Undergraduate, Postgraduate, Diploma and Doctoral programmes. Students can choose programmes according to their interests and career goals.

Some popular programmes include B.Tech, BCA, BBA, MBA, MCA and programmes in Pharmacy, Law and other disciplines. The university also promotes practical and industry-oriented learning to help students develop professional skills.

To learn more about the programmes offered by the university, visit the official GLA University website.

Campus Life

The GLA University campus in Mathura is spread across a large area and provides academic and residential facilities for students. Campus life includes classrooms, laboratories, library facilities, hostels, sports and recreational activities.

Students can also participate in cultural, technical, sports and extracurricular activities. These opportunities help students develop communication, leadership, teamwork and other important skills along with their academic knowledge.
