TECH FEST 2026 - IIT Delhi

(Innovate, Create and Inspire!)

TechFest 2026 at IIT Delhi is an exciting celebration of technology, innovation and creativity! The festival brings together students, innovators, developers and technology enthusiasts to explore new ideas and participate in inspiring technical activities.

( Click the event banner above to visit the official information page and learn more about technology events and activities)

Introduction

TechFest 2026 is a technology-focused college festival inspired by the spirit of innovation and excellence at IIT Delhi. The event provides a platform where students can explore modern technologies, share creative ideas and demonstrate their technical knowledge through exciting activities.

The festival encourages young innovators to think beyond traditional boundaries and work on solutions for real-world challenges. Participants can interact with fellow students, learn from experts and gain valuable experience through workshops, competitions and technical sessions.

Highlights

TechFest 2026 will feature exciting activities for students interested in programming, artificial intelligence, robotics, cybersecurity, cloud computing and software development.

Major highlights include coding competitions, hackathons, robotics challenges, technical quizzes, innovation showcases and interactive workshops. These activities will provide participants with opportunities to test their skills and develop creative solutions.

The festival will also encourage teamwork and collaboration by bringing together students with different technical interests and innovative ideas.

Date and Venue

TechFest 2026 will be organized on: 15 November 2026 from 9:00 AM onwards in Morning.
Venue: IIT Delhi Campus, Hauz Khas, New Delhi.
Participants are encouraged to arrive early to complete the registration process and attend the opening ceremony on time.

Speakers

TechFest 2026 will feature technology professionals, researchers, innovators and experienced speakers who will share their knowledge and experiences with students.

Special sessions will focus on emerging areas such as artificial intelligence, robotics, cybersecurity, cloud computing and entrepreneurship. These sessions will help participants understand current technology trends and future career opportunities.

Registration

Students who want to participate in TechFest 2026 can register online and become part of exciting competitions, workshops and technical activities. Early registration is recommended because participation opportunities may be limited.

Registration deadline: 5 November 2026
