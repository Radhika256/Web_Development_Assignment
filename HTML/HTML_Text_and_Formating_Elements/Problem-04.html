Mathematics Formula Sheet

This Mathematics Formula Sheet contains important formulas from Algebra, Geometry, and Mensuration. These formulas are useful for solving mathematical problems and understanding basic mathematical concepts.

Algebra

Algebra is a branch of mathematics in which letters and symbols are used to represent numbers and quantities. Algebraic formulas help us simplify expressions and solve mathematical problems.

1. Square of a Sum

Formula: (a + b)2 = a2 + 2ab + b2

This formula is used to expand the square of the sum of two numbers or algebraic expressions.

2. Square of a Difference

Formula: (a − b)2 = a2 − 2ab + b2

This formula is used when the difference between two numbers or expressions is squared.

3. Sum of Two Squares

formula: a2 + b2 = (a + b)2 - 2ab

This formula helps in getting whole square term for easier calculation.

4. Difference of Two Squares

Formula: a2 − b2 = (a + b)(a − b)

This formula helps in factorising algebraic expressions containing the difference of two perfect squares.

5. Square of Three Terms

Formula: (a + b + c)2 = a2 + b2 + c2 + 2ab + 2bc + 2ca

This formula is used to expand the square of an expression containing three terms.

6. Cube of a Sum

Formula: (a + b)3 = a3 + 3a2b + 3ab2 + b3

This formula is useful for expanding the cube of the sum of two algebraic terms.

7. Cube of a Difference

Formula: (a − b)3 = a3 − 3a2b + 3ab2 − b3

This formula helps in expanding the cube of the difference between two algebraic expressions.

Geometry

Geometry is the branch of mathematics that deals with shapes, sizes, angles, lines, and properties of figures. Geometry formulas are used to calculate lengths, angles, areas, and other measurements.

1. Area of a Rectangle

Formula: Area = l × b

Here, l represents the length and b represents the breadth of the rectangle.

2. Perimeter of a Rectangle

Formula: Perimeter = 2(l + b)

The perimeter is the total distance around the boundary of a rectangle.

3. Area of a Square

Formula: Area = a2

Here, a represents the length of one side of the square.

4. Perimeter of a Square

Formula: Perimeter = 4a

A square has four equal sides, so its perimeter is four times the length of one side.

5. Area of a Triangle

Formula: Area = 1/2 × b × h

Here, b represents the base of the triangle and h represents its perpendicular height.

6. Pythagorean Theorem

Formula: a2 + b2 = c2

In a right-angled triangle, c represents the hypotenuse, while a and b represent the other two sides.

7. Sum of Angles of a Triangle

Formula: A1 + A2 + A3 = 180°

The sum of the three interior angles of every triangle is 180 degrees.

8. Sum of Angles of a Quadrilateral

Formula: A1 + A2 + A3 + A4 = 360°

The four interior angles of a quadrilateral always add up to 360 degrees.

Mensuration

Mensuration is the branch of mathematics concerned with measuring geometric figures. It includes calculations of perimeter, area, surface area, and volume.

1. Area of a Circle

Formula: Area = πr2

Here, r represents the radius of the circle. The value of π is approximately 22/7 or 3.14.

2. Circumference of a Circle

Formula: Circumference = 2πr

The circumference is the distance around the boundary of a circle.

3. Area of a Square

Formula: Area = a2

The area of a square is obtained by multiplying its side by itself.

4. Volume of a Cube

Formula: Volume = a3

Here, a represents the length of one edge of the cube. Volume is measured in cubic units such as cm3 and m3.

5. Total Surface Area of a Cube

Formula: Total Surface Area = 6a2

A cube has six equal square faces. Therefore, its total surface area is six times the area of one face.

6. Volume of a Cuboid

Formula: Volume = l × b × h

Here, l represents length, b represents breadth, and h represents height.

7. Total Surface Area of a Cuboid

Formula: Total Surface Area = 2(lb + bh + hl)

This formula is used to calculate the total area of all six faces of a cuboid.

8. Volume of a Cylinder

Formula: Volume = πr2h

Here, r is the radius of the circular base and h is the height of the cylinder.

9. Curved Surface Area of a Cylinder

Formula: Curved Surface Area = 2πrh

This formula gives the area of the curved portion of a cylinder, excluding its two circular bases.

10. Volume of a Sphere

Formula: Volume = 4/3 πr3

Here, r represents the radius of the sphere. The unit of volume can be cm3, m3, or another cubic unit.
