GLA University Information Portal

Welcome to the University Information Portal of GLA University, Mathura. GLA University provides professional education and focuses on academic excellence, research, innovation, and the overall development of students.

About University

GLA University is located in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh. Its vision is to become a pace-setting university focused on academic excellence in education, research, and development in established and emerging professions.

The university aims to provide quality professional education and promote the overall growth of students. It follows a student-centric approach and encourages intellectual and personality development.

Education, research, innovation, and student development are important areas of focus at GLA University.

Departments

GLA University has academic departments in different fields. These include Computer Engineering and Applications, Electrical Engineering, Electronics and Communication, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, English, Biotechnology, and Agricultural Sciences.

The university also has institutes for Engineering and Technology, Business Management, Pharmaceutical Research, Applied Sciences and Humanities, Legal Studies and Research, and other areas of education.

These departments provide students with subject knowledge and help them develop practical and professional skills.

Academic Programs

GLA University offers graduate, postgraduate, doctoral, and diploma programmes. The university provides more than 70 multidisciplinary programmes in different fields of study.

Some academic programmes include B.Tech, MBA, MCA, M.Tech, M.Sc., M.Pharm, LLM, Ph.D., and diploma programmes.

Scientific subjects use subscripts in formulas such as H2O and CO2. Mathematical expressions use superscripts, such as x2 and 103.

Campus Facilities

GLA University has a campus spread across 110 acres and serves more than 20,000 students enrolled in different professional courses.

The campus includes contemporary laboratories, residential complexes, libraries, hostels, sports facilities, and other recreational and academic facilities.

The campus environment supports academic learning as well as extracurricular, co-curricular, and athletic activities.

Important Information

Students should attend classes regularly, complete academic work on time, and follow university rules and regulations. Discipline and academic honesty are important for a successful university experience.

GLA University also supports experiential and project-based education, helping students connect academic concepts with practical learning.
