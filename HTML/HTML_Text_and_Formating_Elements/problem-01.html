GLA University

About the College

Ganeshi Lal Agrawal (GLA) University is a private university in Mathura, established in 2010. GLA University Mathura also has a NAAC accreditation with an A+ grade. The university is approved by various authorities, including AICTE, UGC, NCTE, the Bar Council of India, ICAR, etc. The institute has been ranked 101-150out of 200 in India in the NIRF Overall Ranking 2025. GLA University Mathura has been ranked 31 in India for education by Collegedunia in 2025. GLA University Mathura offers UG, PG, PhD, and diploma courses. GLA University offers B.Tech as its flagship program. The B.Tech program's 1st-year fee ranges from INR 1.85 Lakhs to INR 2.40 Lakhs, depending on the specialization.

Courses Offered

GLA University Mathura courses cover many disciplines- Management, Business, Law, Technology, Humanities, etc. It offers undergraduate, postgraduate, doctorate, and diploma courses. The most popular GLA University courses are: BTech, MBA, BALLB and BBA LLB.

Campus Life

Spread across 110 acres of land, the university is home to over 20,000 students enrolled in a variety of professional courses. Well-designed and maintained buildings, contemporary laboratories, spacious residential complexes and recreational facilities make the campus one of the best in the region and gives its students an edge over their counterparts coming from other universities. The diverse student population makes it hard to describe the typical student and even harder to describe the quintessential GLA student experience. GLA is committed to making educational opportunities accessible to all by granting scholarships and financial aid to its students. Extracurricular, co-curricular and athletic opportunities, in addition to academics, GLA students are active around and beyond campus.

Why I Like My College

Brief Description of My College

GLA University, Mathura is a private university focused on technical education, discipline, and skill-based learning. The campus offers good academic infrastructure, libraries, labs, and a structured environment that supports students in building strong fundamentals and professional discipline.

Environment

The college environment is disciplined and academically focused. Faculty members are supportive toward students who are genuinely interested in learning. Peer interaction and healthy competition motivate students to push their limits and improve consistently.

College Fests, Events & Hackathons

GLA University conducts cultural and technical fests that encourage student participation beyond academics. Hackathons, coding contests, and technical events provide hands-on exposure, improve teamwork, and enhance problem-solving and communication skills.

Placements

The placement cell actively works to provide opportunities for students. Many companies visit the campus every year across different technical roles.

My first year was mainly about adapting to college life and understanding the academic system. Everything was new — subjects, exams, teaching methods, and peer competition. During this phase, I explored different domains in computer science but lacked proper direction. I focused more on completing coursework than building strong technical skills. This has taught me the importance of time management and self-awareness.
