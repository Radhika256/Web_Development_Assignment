Student Terms & Rules

(This page contains the important terms, rules, and notices that every student is expected to follow during the academic session. Students should read all sections carefully and follow the instructions given below.)

General Rules

1. Students must carry their valid student identity card while on the institution's premises. The identity card may be required for entry into classrooms, laboratories, libraries, and examination halls.

2. Students must attend classes regularly and follow the instructions given by teachers and institutional staff. Students are responsible for keeping themselves informed about academic activities and notices.

3. Students should maintain cleanliness in classrooms, laboratories, libraries, corridors, and other common areas.
Students are encouraged to use designated waste bins and help maintain a clean and healthy learning environment.

4. Mobile phones and other electronic devices must be kept on silent mode during classes unless their use has been specifically permitted by the teacher.

Attendance

5. Students are required to maintain a minimum attendance of 75%. Attendance will be recorded for every scheduled class, and students should regularly check their attendance status.

6. Students who are absent due to a genuine reason should inform the concerned teacher or institution as soon as possible and provide supporting documents when required.

Students falling below the required attendance percentage may be restricted from appearing in the final examination. Exceptional cases, if applicable, will be considered according to institutional rules.

Examination

7. Students must report to the examination hall at least 15 minutes before the scheduled examination time. Students should bring their identity card and other permitted examination materials.

8. Students must follow all examination instructions given by the invigilator. Any unauthorized material, communication, or electronic device may be treated as a violation of examination rules.

Students arriving late may not be allowed additional time to complete the examination. The purpose of this rule is to ensure fairness and maintain proper examination conditions for all students.

Assignment Submission

9. All assignments must be submitted through the method specified by the concerned teacher. Students should ensure that their name, class, roll number, subject, and assignment details are clearly mentioned.

Assignments must be submitted by the deadline announced by the teacher. For example, an assignment announced on Monday with a submission deadline of Friday must be submitted no later than Friday.

10. Students must submit their own original work. Copying another student's work or submitting material without proper acknowledgment may be treated as academic misconduct.

Late submissions may receive a penalty or may not be accepted after the permitted submission period. Students should submit assignments early enough to avoid problems caused by technical issues or other unexpected circumstances.

Discipline

11. Students must behave respectfully toward teachers, staff members, visitors, and fellow students. Offensive language, threatening behavior, harassment, or deliberate disruption of academic activities is not permitted.

12. Students must not damage institutional property. Any student found intentionally damaging furniture, equipment, books, computers, or other property may be required to compensate for the damage.

Serious or repeated violations of disciplinary rules may result in suspension, cancellation of privileges, or other institutional penalties.

Disciplinary action will depend on the nature and seriousness of the violation and will be handled according to the applicable institutional procedures.

Important Notice

Students are responsible for reading institutional notices and completing all academic requirements within the specified time. Important information may include changes to class schedules, examination dates, assignment deadlines, attendance requirements, and submission instructions.

Always verify the latest deadline or examination notice before making a submission or appearing for an examination. This helps students avoid missed deadlines and preventable penalties.

Failure to read or follow these rules does not normally exempt a student from the applicable requirements.
