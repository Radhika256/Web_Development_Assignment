Science Notes

Chemistry

Chemistry is the study of matter, its properties, composition, and chemical changes.

Important Chemical Formulas

1. Water: H2O
Water contains two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom.

2. Carbon Dioxide: CO2
Carbon dioxide contains one carbon atom and two oxygen atoms.

3. Oxygen: O2
Oxygen gas contains two oxygen atoms.

4. Sulfuric Acid: H2SO4
Sulfuric acid contains hydrogen, sulfur, and oxygen.

5. Ammonia: NH3
Ammonia contains one nitrogen atom and three hydrogen atoms.

Physics

Physics is the study of matter, energy, motion, force, and interactions.

Important Physics Formulas

1. Speed: v = d / t
Speed is the distance travelled divided by the time taken.

2. Force: F = m × a
Force is equal to mass multiplied by acceleration.

3. Work: W = F × d
Work is done when a force moves an object through a distance.

4. Area: m2
Area is measured in square metres.

5. Volume: m3
Volume is measured in cubic metres.

Scientific notation: 103, 106, and 10-3 are examples of powers of ten.

Biology

Biology is the study of living organisms, their structure, functions, growth, and environment.

Important Biological Formulas and Equations

1. Photosynthesis: 6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2
Green plants use carbon dioxide and water in the presence of sunlight to produce glucose and oxygen.

2. Cellular Respiration: C6H12O6 + 6O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O + Energy
Cells break down glucose using oxygen to release energy.

3. Population Density: Population Density = Number of Organisms / Area
It represents the number of organisms living in a particular area.

4. Magnification: Magnification = Image Size / Actual Size
Magnification tells us how much larger an image is compared with the actual object.

5. BMI: BMI = Mass (kg) / Height2 (m2)
BMI is a mathematical measure based on body mass and height.
