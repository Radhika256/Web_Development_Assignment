Bank Accounts

Savings Account

A savings account helps you safely keep money while earning interest. It is suitable for emergency funds and regular savings.

Tip: Maintain an emergency fund before taking unnecessary loans.

Current Account

Current accounts are mainly designed for businesses and frequent transactions. They generally provide convenient payment facilities.

  1. Choose a suitable account.
  2. Complete the application form.
  3. Submit required KYC documents.
  4. Complete verification.
  5. Activate your account and banking services.

Credit & Debit Cards

Debit Card

A debit card allows you to spend money directly from your bank account.

Credit Card

A credit card provides a credit limit. Always pay bills on time to avoid interest and additional charges.

Understanding Loans

Personal Loan

A personal loan can be used for eligible personal expenses. Compare interest rates, processing fees and repayment periods before borrowing.

Home Loan

A home loan helps eligible customers finance a residential property through structured monthly repayments.

Interest Rate
The percentage charged on the borrowed amount.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment paid regularly towards a loan.
Principal
The original amount borrowed from the lender.
Tenure
The total period available for repaying the loan.
"Do not borrow simply because credit is available. Borrow according to your repayment capacity."

Responsible Finance Tips

  • Create and follow a monthly budget.
  • Save regularly for emergencies.
  • Pay credit-card bills and EMIs on time.
  • Compare interest rates and charges.
  • Avoid unnecessary debt.
  • Protect your PIN, passwords and banking information.
  • Review your bank statements regularly.
  • Understand loan terms before signing an agreement.

Financial examples: ₹10,000 + ₹5,000 = ₹15,000 | Interest = Principal × Rate × Time | Budget ≤ Income
