Recruitment Process

Our hiring workflow is designed to be transparent, efficient, and respectful of your time.

  1. Application Review
  2. Initial Screening
  3. Technical Assessment / Interview
  4. HR & Culture Fit Round
  5. Offer Extended

Detailed Stage Descriptions

Screening
A brief 30-minute call with our recruiter to discuss your background, career goals, and alignment with Google values.
Technical Round
An evaluation of your core skills through a technical assignment, system design discussion, or live coding session.
HR Round
A conversation focused on leadership capabilities, compensation expectations, team dynamics, and long-term vision.
Offer
Official delivery of your formal job offer package along with onboarding documentation and team welcomes.
