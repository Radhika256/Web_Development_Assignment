About the Career Expo

The National Tech & Career Expo 2026 brings students, graduates, technology companies, educators and industry professionals together under one roof. The event focuses on career opportunities, internships, workshops and professional networking.

"Your career grows when curiosity meets opportunity."

Explore opportunities at Microsoft, Google, IBM and TCS.

Building the Future Together

Our goal is to provide students with direct access to industry experts, career guidance and practical learning. Participants can attend workshops, meet recruiters and discover pathways into the technology industry.

Expo Quick Tips

  • Carry multiple copies of your resume.
  • Research companies before visiting their booths.
  • Prepare a short self-introduction.
  • Ask meaningful questions to recruiters.
  • Keep your LinkedIn profile updated.
  • Attend workshops and networking sessions.

Attendee Journey

  1. Register for the Expo.
  2. Receive your attendee confirmation.
  3. Check the event schedule.
  4. Attend the opening session.
  5. Visit company booths.
  6. Join workshops and speaker sessions.
  7. Network with recruiters and professionals.
  8. Apply for suitable opportunities.
  9. Collect certificates and resources.
  10. Follow up with companies after the event.

Important Expo Terms

Career Expo
An event where students and professionals meet organizations and explore careers.
Internship
A short-term professional learning opportunity for students or graduates.
Placement
A recruitment opportunity that can lead to full-time employment.
Workshop
An interactive session designed to develop practical skills.
Certification
Recognition that a participant has completed a course or learning activity.
Networking
Building professional relationships with people from the industry.
