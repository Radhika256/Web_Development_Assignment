Welcome to Oori

Oori means "our village" in Kannada. The restaurant celebrates Indian food traditions, fresh ingredients and warm hospitality.

Our Story

Oori brings together traditional recipes and carefully selected ingredients to create a memorable dining experience. It is a place for family meals, friends, celebrations and special occasions.
