Leave Categories and Guidelines

Leave Category Description Purpose Typical Duration Supporting Document
Casual Leave Leave taken for personal or unexpected short-term needs. Personal work, family events or urgent matters. 1–3 days Usually Not Required
Sick Leave Leave taken when an employee is unwell or needs medical rest. Illness, treatment or medical recovery. 1–7 days Medical Certificate if required
Earned Leave Planned leave accumulated by an employee during employment. Vacation, travel or personal activities. 3–30 days Usually Not Required
Work From Home Employee works from their home instead of the office. Personal situations or approved remote work. 1–5 days Not Required
Other Used when the requested leave does not fit into the listed categories. Special or exceptional circumstances. As Approved Depends on Reason
